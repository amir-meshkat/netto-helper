From bruto to netto for your household, 2026

Pick a situation, type the gross monthly salary of each job, and see what the household keeps after pension and income tax. Figures are for a full year of 2026, for people below AOW age.

Situation

Pension: every employer has its own pension scheme. Find your own premium percentage and the franchise (the part of your salary with no pension build-up) in your pension regulation or on your payslip, and enter them per job. Leave them at 0 to ignore pension.

Household netto

Two jobs. During the year only one employer applies the loonheffingskorting (the tax credits), so the second job withholds more tax per month. What you see here is the final yearly result, as it would come out of your aangifte. The difference between monthly withholding and this result is settled afterwards, or earlier through a voorlopige aanslag.

Couples. Salaries are taxed per person. Being fiscal partners does not change the numbers here; it starts to matter for shared items such as mortgage interest, savings in box 3, and deductions.

Not included yet. Toeslagen, mortgage interest and other deductions, box 3 savings, lijfrente, bonuses with special rates, and anyone at AOW age. The employer pays the Zvw health contribution, so it is not taken from your salary. Indicative only, rounding can differ a few euros from the Belastingdienst.
